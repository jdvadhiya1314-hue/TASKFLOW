# Problem Statement

In many small organizations, project groups, and academic environments, task management is handled informally using disjointed tools such as email threads, instant messaging, and static spreadsheets. This decentralized approach introduces several critical operational issues:

- **Lack of Centralization:** Without a single source of truth, assignments are easily lost or misinterpreted, leading to duplicated effort or dropped responsibilities.
- **Poor Deadline Management:** Manual tracking of due dates often results in missed deadlines. There is no automated way to highlight overdue tasks or alert users to impending due dates.
- **Informal Postponement Handling:** When a user cannot meet a deadline, extensions are negotiated informally. This lacks a formal approval process, making it difficult to track why a deadline was missed or who authorized the extension.
- **Ignored Dependencies:** Tasks often need to be executed in a specific sequence. Informal systems cannot enforce these prerequisites, allowing users to start work prematurely, which can cause downstream errors or require rework.
- **Lack of Real-Time Notifications:** Participants are not immediately aware when they are assigned new work, or when a request (like a deadline extension) is approved or rejected, causing unnecessary delays.
- **No Audit Trail:** It is often impossible to reconstruct the history of a task to determine who made a change, when a status was updated, or what discussions occurred.

TASKFLOW addresses these challenges directly by providing a structured, centralized system that enforces task lifecycles, manages dependencies, formalizes postponement requests, and automatically maintains an immutable audit trail of all project activities.
